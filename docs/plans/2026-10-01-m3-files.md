# M3 — Fichiers : plan d'implémentation

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ne plus rien perdre. Il s'agit de choisir son projet sur une page d'accueil (projets récents, nouveau, ouvrir), d'enregistrer et d'ouvrir depuis un menu Fichier, avec une sauvegarde automatique et sa restauration après un plantage, une confirmation avant de perdre des modifications, le titre de fenêtre marqué `*` et des messages d'erreur clairs.

**Architecture:**
- **`ProjectStore`** (autoload) apprend à créer, ouvrir, restaurer et enregistrer un projet. L'écriture passe par un fichier temporaire, puis un renommage. Le store suit les modifications non enregistrées en comparant la version de l'`UndoRedo` à celle du dernier enregistrement. Il émet `edited`, `dirty_changed` et `path_changed`.
- **`Autosave`** (nœud) écrit une copie dans `user://` 2 s après la dernière modification et la supprime dès que le projet redevient propre.
- **`WelcomeScreen`** (Control) est la page d'accueil. Elle n'émet que des choix : projet récent, oubli d'un projet, nouveau, ouvrir, restaurer ou ignorer.
- **`FileController`** (nœud) porte le comportement du menu Fichier :
  - la page d'accueil ;
  - les dialogues : confirmation, erreur, sélection de fichier native ;
  - le titre de la fenêtre ;
  - la liste des projets récents.

  Les dialogues natifs ne sont pas pilotables dans les tests, donc le sélecteur de fichier et l'action de fermeture sont injectables.
- **`main.gd`** assemble le tout. Il bloque la fermeture automatique de la fenêtre et route ⌘O, ⌘S et ⌘⇧S.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2, GDScript, GUT 9.7.1.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-09-30-v1-design.md` (§8 Fichiers et sauvegarde, §8 Erreurs, §11 jalon M3).

## Global Constraints

- Godot **4.7.2**, GDScript uniquement. GUT **9.7.1**.
- Indentation par **tabulations**.
- Code et commentaires en anglais. **Libellés, dialogues et messages en français.**
- Les vues ne modifient jamais les données : tout passe par `ProjectStore` (spec §3).
- Connexions de signaux vers l'autoload : des **méthodes liées**, pas des lambdas.
- Les **tests n'écrivent jamais dans les vrais fichiers de l'utilisateur** : préférences, sauvegarde automatique et projets passent par des chemins `user://test_…` injectés.
- Tests : `./scripts/test.sh`, ou `./scripts/test.sh -gselect=<fichier>` pour un seul fichier.
- Les `.uid` générés par Godot sont commités avec leur source.
- Branche `feat/m3-files`, PR vers `develop` assignée à `@me`.
- Commits au format Conventional Commits, **sans** trailer `Co-Authored-By`, sans lien de session et sans pied de page « Generated with ».

## Choix et écarts par rapport à la spec

1. **« Sauvegarde automatique plus récente que le dernier enregistrement »** (spec §8) : la copie est supprimée à chaque enregistrement, et aussi quand on revient à l'état enregistré ou qu'on ouvre un autre projet. Sa seule présence au lancement signifie donc qu'il y a du travail non enregistré, et aucune comparaison de dates n'est nécessaire. Les métadonnées (fichier d'origine, date) sont dans `user://autosave.cfg`.
2. **Au lancement** (demande de l'utilisateur), une page d'accueil propose :
   - les 8 derniers projets, avec un projet introuvable grisé et une croix pour le retirer ;
   - « Nouveau projet vide », « Nouveau depuis le log 16 » et « Ouvrir un fichier… » ;
   - une carte « Restaurer / Ignorer » s'il reste du travail non enregistré.

   La page est rouvrable par Fichier › Accueil… et ⌘⇧O. Échap la ferme dès qu'un projet est ouvert. La question « Enregistrer les modifications ? » est posée au moment de remplacer le projet, pas à l'ouverture de la page.
3. **« Ne pas enregistrer »** supprime aussi la sauvegarde automatique, pour ne pas reproposer au lancement suivant un travail volontairement abandonné.
4. **Menu natif** : sur macOS, le menu Fichier est dans la barre de menus du système (`MenuBar.prefer_global_menu`). Ailleurs, il est en haut au centre de la fenêtre. Les raccourcis ne sont pas déclarés dans le menu mais gérés par `Shortcuts`, pour éviter un double déclenchement.
5. **Identifiants** : les ids des fichiers nomment des nœuds de scène, donc `ProjectValidator` exige désormais `^[A-Za-z0-9_-]+$`. L'aperçu de pose s'appelle « Ghost preview » (avec une espace) et ne peut donc jamais entrer en collision avec un meuble.
6. **Migrations de schéma et GLB introuvable** (spec §8) : il n'existe qu'un schéma (v1) et l'import GLB arrive en M6, donc rien à faire en M3.

## Review Focus

1. **Écriture impossible** (dossier absent ou en lecture seule) : un message l'explique, le projet reste marqué modifié, le chemin ne change pas et la sauvegarde automatique est conservée. Tests : `test_failed_write_keeps_project_dirty_and_path_unchanged` (Task 2) et `test_failed_save_shows_why_and_keeps_changes` (Task 6).
2. **Fichier ouvert illisible** : la raison s'affiche et le projet courant reste intact. Test : `test_open_of_a_broken_file_shows_the_reason_and_keeps_the_project` (Task 6).
3. **Dialogue d'enregistrement annulé pendant la confirmation** : on ne continue pas, et rien n'est perdu. Test : `test_save_cancelled_during_confirmation_does_not_continue` (Task 6).
4. **Projet récent supprimé du disque** : la page le grise. S'il est quand même ouvert, la raison s'affiche et la page reste ouverte. Tests : `test_missing_project_is_disabled_and_marked` (Task 5) et `test_vanished_recent_project_reports_it_and_keeps_the_page` (Task 6).
5. **Identifiant piégé dans un fichier** (`/`, `:`, espace, « Ghost preview ») : le fichier est refusé à l'ouverture. Test : `test_rejects_ids_that_are_not_plain_names` (Task 1).

```gdscript
const Store := preload("res://src/store/project_store.gd")

var _tool: FurnitureTool


func before_each() -> void:
	_store = autofree(Store.new())

	_store.project.furniture.clear()
	_view = FurnitureView.new()

	_tool = FurnitureTool.new()
	add_child_autofree(_tool)
	_tool.setup(_store, _view, _camera)


func _screen(pos: Vector2, height := 0.0) -> Vector2:
	return _camera.unproject_position(Vector3(pos.x, height, pos.y))


func _move(pos: Vector2, alt := false, held := false) -> void:
	var event := InputEventMouseMotion.new()
	event.position = _screen(pos)
	event.alt_pressed = alt
	event.button_mask = MOUSE_BUTTON_MASK_LEFT if held else 0
	_tool._unhandled_input(event)


func _click(pos: Vector2, pressed := true, height := 0.0) -> void:
	var event := InputEventMouseButton.new()
	event.button_index = MOUSE_BUTTON_LEFT
	event.pressed = pressed
	event.position = _screen(pos, height)
	_tool._unhandled_input(event)


func _key(keycode: Key, command := false, shift := false) -> void:
	var event := InputEventKey.new()
	event.keycode = keycode
	event.pressed = true
	# Cmd on macOS, Ctrl elsewhere: set both so the test runs on any platform.
	event.ctrl_pressed = command
	event.meta_pressed = command
	event.shift_pressed = shift
	_tool._unhandled_input(event)


func _wheel(up: bool, shift: bool, horizontal := false) -> void:
	var event := InputEventMouseButton.new()
	if horizontal:
		event.button_index = MOUSE_BUTTON_WHEEL_LEFT if up else MOUSE_BUTTON_WHEEL_RIGHT
	else:
		event.button_index = MOUSE_BUTTON_WHEEL_UP if up else MOUSE_BUTTON_WHEEL_DOWN
	event.pressed = true
	event.shift_pressed = shift
	_tool._unhandled_input(event)

	var item := Fixtures.sofa(_store.next_furniture_id())
	item.pos = pos
	item.rotation_deg = rotation_deg
```

```gdscript
class_name ProjectValidator
extends RefCounted
## Checks the invariants a loaded or edited project must satisfy.
## Messages are shown to the user as-is, so they are in French.

## Ids name scene nodes, so they must stay plain: letters, digits, "_" and "-".
const ID_PATTERN := "^[A-Za-z0-9_-]+$"

static var _id_regex := RegEx.create_from_string(ID_PATTERN)

## Tolerance for float noise when an opening sits exactly at a wall end.
const LENGTH_EPSILON := 1e-4


## Returns one message per violated invariant; empty when the project is valid.
static func validate(project: ProjectData) -> PackedStringArray:
	var errors := PackedStringArray()
	if project.wall_height <= 0.0:
		errors.append("projet : la hauteur des murs doit être positive")
	if project.underlay != null and project.underlay.meters_per_px <= 0.0:
		errors.append("calque : l'échelle doit être positive")
	_check_unique_ids("nœud", project.nodes, errors)
	_check_unique_ids("mur", project.walls, errors)
	_check_unique_ids("ouverture", project.openings, errors)
	_check_unique_ids("pièce", project.rooms, errors)
	_check_unique_ids("meuble", project.furniture, errors)
	for wall in project.walls:
		_check_wall(project, wall, errors)
	for opening in project.openings:
		_check_opening(project, opening, errors)
	for room in project.rooms:
		_check_room(project, room, errors)
	for item in project.furniture:
		_check_furniture(item, errors)
	return errors


static func _check_unique_ids(label: String, entities: Array, errors: PackedStringArray) -> void:
	var seen := {}
	for entity in entities:
		if _id_regex.search(entity.id) == null:
			errors.append("%s « %s » : identifiant invalide (lettres, chiffres, _ et - uniquement)" % [label, entity.id])
		if seen.has(entity.id):
			errors.append("%s « %s » : identifiant en double" % [label, entity.id])
		seen[entity.id] = true


static func _check_wall(project: ProjectData, wall: Wall, errors: PackedStringArray) -> void:
	var prefix := "mur « %s »" % wall.id
	for node_id in [wall.a, wall.b]:
		if project.get_plan_node(node_id) == null:
			errors.append("%s : nœud inconnu « %s »" % [prefix, node_id])
	if wall.a == wall.b:
		errors.append("%s : relie un nœud à lui-même" % prefix)
	if wall.thickness <= 0.0:
		errors.append("%s : l'épaisseur doit être positive" % prefix)
	if wall.height <= 0.0:
		errors.append("%s : la hauteur doit être positive" % prefix)


static func _check_opening(project: ProjectData, opening: Opening, errors: PackedStringArray) -> void:
	var prefix := "ouverture « %s »" % opening.id
	if not opening.kind in Opening.KINDS:
		errors.append("%s : type inconnu « %s »" % [prefix, opening.kind])
	if not opening.swing in Opening.SWINGS:
		errors.append("%s : sens d'ouverture inconnu « %s »" % [prefix, opening.swing])
	if opening.width <= 0.0 or opening.height <= 0.0:
		errors.append("%s : largeur et hauteur doivent être positives" % prefix)
	if opening.sill < 0.0:
		errors.append("%s : l'allège ne peut pas être négative" % prefix)
	var wall := project.get_wall(opening.wall_id)
	if wall == null:
		errors.append("%s : mur inconnu « %s »" % [prefix, opening.wall_id])
		return
	var length := project.wall_length(wall)
	if length < 0.0:
		return  # The wall itself is already reported as broken.
	var half_width := opening.width / 2.0
	if opening.offset - half_width < -LENGTH_EPSILON or opening.offset + half_width > length + LENGTH_EPSILON:
		errors.append("%s : dépasse du mur « %s » (longueur %.2f m)" % [prefix, wall.id, length])


static func _check_room(project: ProjectData, room: Room, errors: PackedStringArray) -> void:
	var prefix := "pièce « %s »" % room.id
	if room.node_ids.size() < 3:
		errors.append("%s : il faut au moins 3 nœuds" % prefix)
	for node_id in room.node_ids:
		if project.get_plan_node(node_id) == null:
			errors.append("%s : nœud inconnu « %s »" % [prefix, node_id])
	if not room.floor_kind in Room.FLOOR_KINDS:
		errors.append("%s : type de sol inconnu « %s »" % [prefix, room.floor_kind])


static func _check_furniture(item: FurnitureItem, errors: PackedStringArray) -> void:
	if item.size.x <= 0.0 or item.size.y <= 0.0 or item.size.z <= 0.0:
		errors.append("meuble « %s » : les dimensions doivent être positives" % item.id)
```

```gdscript
class_name FurnitureView
extends Node3D
## Draws furniture items as coloured blocks, the selection outline, and the
## translucent ghost used while placing a new item. Never edits the project.

## Contains a space, which ProjectValidator forbids in ids, so it never clashes with an item.
const GHOST_NAME := "Ghost preview"

var _selected_id := ""
var _outline := Palette.outline_material()
var _store: Node


## Keeps the blocks in sync with a ProjectStore (the autoload, or a test double).
## Bound methods (not lambdas) so the connections die with this node.
func bind_store(store: Node) -> void:
	_store = store
	store.project_loaded.connect(_on_project_loaded)
	store.furniture_added.connect(_on_furniture_added)
	store.furniture_changed.connect(_on_furniture_changed)
	store.furniture_removed.connect(remove_item)
	_on_project_loaded()


func _on_project_loaded() -> void:
	show_items(_store.project.furniture)


func _on_furniture_added(id: String) -> void:
	add_item(_store.get_furniture(id))


func _on_furniture_changed(id: String) -> void:
	update_item(_store.get_furniture(id))


func show_items(items: Array[FurnitureItem]) -> void:
	for child in get_children():
		remove_child(child)
		child.queue_free()
	_selected_id = ""
	for item in items:
		add_item(item)


func add_item(item: FurnitureItem) -> void:
	var node := _block(item.size, Palette.material(item.color))
	node.name = item.id
	add_child(node)
	_place(node, item.pos, item.rotation_deg)


## Rebuilds the item's block from its current data.
func update_item(item: FurnitureItem) -> void:
	var node := get_node_or_null(NodePath(item.id)) as Node3D
	if node == null:
		return
	var box := node.get_node("Box") as MeshInstance3D
	(box.mesh as BoxMesh).size = item.size
	box.position.y = item.size.y / 2.0
	(box.material_override as StandardMaterial3D).albedo_color = item.color
	_place(node, item.pos, item.rotation_deg)


func remove_item(id: String) -> void:
	var node := get_node_or_null(NodePath(id))
	if node == null:
		return
	if id == _selected_id:
		_selected_id = ""
	remove_child(node)
	node.queue_free()


## Moves the item's block without touching the data (live drag feedback).
func preview_item(id: String, pos: Vector2, rotation_deg: float) -> void:
	var node := get_node_or_null(NodePath(id)) as Node3D
	if node != null:
		_place(node, pos, rotation_deg)


## Outlines the item with this id; "" or an unknown id clears the selection.
func set_selected(id: String) -> void:
	if _selected_id != "":
		_set_outline(_selected_id, false)
	_selected_id = id if has_node(NodePath(id)) and id != GHOST_NAME else ""
	if _selected_id != "":
		_set_outline(_selected_id, true)


func selected_id() -> String:
	return _selected_id


func show_ghost(size: Vector3, color: Color) -> void:
	hide_ghost()
	var ghost_color := color
	ghost_color.a = Palette.GHOST_ALPHA
	var ghost := _block(size, Palette.material(ghost_color))
	ghost.name = GHOST_NAME
	add_child(ghost)


func move_ghost(pos: Vector2, rotation_deg: float) -> void:
	var ghost := get_node_or_null(GHOST_NAME) as Node3D
	if ghost != null:
		_place(ghost, pos, rotation_deg)


func hide_ghost() -> void:
	var ghost := get_node_or_null(GHOST_NAME)
	if ghost != null:
		remove_child(ghost)
		ghost.queue_free()


func has_ghost() -> bool:
	return get_node_or_null(GHOST_NAME) != null


func _set_outline(id: String, on: bool) -> void:
	var node := get_node_or_null(NodePath(id))
	if node == null:
		return
	var mat := (node.get_node("Box") as MeshInstance3D).material_override as StandardMaterial3D
	mat.next_pass = _outline if on else null


static func _block(size: Vector3, material: StandardMaterial3D) -> Node3D:
	var node := Node3D.new()
	var box := MeshInstance3D.new()
	box.name = "Box"
	var mesh := BoxMesh.new()
	mesh.size = size
	box.mesh = mesh
	box.material_override = material
	box.position.y = size.y / 2.0
	node.add_child(box)
	return node


static func _place(node: Node3D, pos: Vector2, rotation_deg: float) -> void:
	node.position = Vector3(pos.x, 0, pos.y)
	node.rotation = Vector3(0, deg_to_rad(rotation_deg), 0)
```

```gdscript
signal edited
signal dirty_changed(dirty: bool)
signal path_changed(path: String)
signal furniture_added(id: String)
signal furniture_changed(id: String)
signal furniture_removed(id: String)

## Fields of a FurnitureItem that update_furniture accepts.
const EDITABLE_FIELDS: PackedStringArray = ["name", "size", "color", "pos", "rotation_deg"]
## Offset of a duplicate from its original, so both stay visible.
const DUPLICATE_OFFSET := Vector2(0.3, 0.3)

var project: ProjectData = ProjectData.new()
## File the project was opened from or last saved to; "" for a new project.
var current_path := ""
var _history := UndoRedo.new()
## History version at the last open or save; -1 when restored unsaved work.
var _saved_version := 0


func _init() -> void:
	_saved_version = _history.get_version()
	_history.version_changed.connect(_on_version_changed)


func _notification(what: int) -> void:
	if what == NOTIFICATION_PREDELETE:
		_history.free()


## True when the project has changes that are not saved to a file.
func is_dirty() -> bool:
	return _history.get_version() != _saved_version


## Replaces the open project with an empty one.
func new_project() -> void:
	_replace(ProjectData.new(), "")


## Replaces the open project with the file's content. With `as_template`, the
## file is only a starting point: the project stays untitled, so saving asks
## for a new path. On failure the current project is kept and the reasons are
## returned.
func load_from_file(path: String, as_template: bool = false) -> PackedStringArray:
	if not FileAccess.file_exists(path):
		return PackedStringArray(["fichier introuvable : %s" % path])
	var result := ProjectSerializer.from_json(FileAccess.get_file_as_string(path))
	if not result.is_ok():
		return result.errors
	_replace(result.project, "" if as_template else path)
	return PackedStringArray()


## Reopens unsaved work (the autosave) as a modified version of `original_path`.
func restore_from_text(text: String, original_path: String) -> PackedStringArray:
	var result := ProjectSerializer.from_json(text)
	if not result.is_ok():
		return result.errors
	_replace(result.project, original_path)
	_saved_version = -1
	dirty_changed.emit(true)
	return PackedStringArray()


## Writes the project to `path` (through a temporary file, so a failed write
## never truncates an existing project) and makes it the current file. On
## failure nothing changes and the reasons are returned.
func save_to_file(path: String) -> PackedStringArray:
	var invalid := ProjectValidator.validate(project)
	if not invalid.is_empty():
		return invalid
	var error := _write_atomically(path, ProjectSerializer.to_json(project))
	if error != OK:
		return PackedStringArray(["écriture impossible : %s (%s)" % [path, error_string(error)]])
	current_path = path
	_saved_version = _history.get_version()
	path_changed.emit(path)
	dirty_changed.emit(false)
	return PackedStringArray()


func get_furniture(id: String) -> FurnitureItem:
	for item in project.furniture:
		if item.id == id:
			return item
	return null


## "f" followed by one more than the highest numbered "f<n>" id in use.
func next_furniture_id() -> String:
	var highest := 0
	for item in project.furniture:
		var suffix := item.id.substr(1)
		if item.id.begins_with("f") and suffix.is_valid_int():
			highest = maxi(highest, suffix.to_int())
	return "f%d" % (highest + 1)


## Adds a copy of `item`. Returns false (and changes nothing) if the item is
## invalid or its id is already used.
func add_furniture(item: FurnitureItem) -> bool:
	if get_furniture(item.id) != null or not _is_valid(item):
		return false
	var stored := item.copy()
	_history.create_action("Ajouter %s" % stored.name)
	_history.add_do_method(_insert.bind(stored, project.furniture.size()))
	_history.add_undo_method(_delete.bind(stored.id))
	_history.commit_action()
	return true


## Applies `changes` (keys from EDITABLE_FIELDS) to the item. Returns false
## (and changes nothing) for an unknown id, unknown field or invalid result.
func update_furniture(id: String, changes: Dictionary) -> bool:
	var item := get_furniture(id)
	if item == null or changes.is_empty():
		return false
	var previous := {}
	var preview := item.copy()
	for key in changes:
		if not key in EDITABLE_FIELDS:
			return false
		previous[key] = item.get(key)
		preview.set(key, changes[key])
	if not _is_valid(preview):
		return false
	_history.create_action("Modifier %s" % item.name)
	_history.add_do_method(_apply.bind(id, changes.duplicate()))
	_history.add_undo_method(_apply.bind(id, previous))
	_history.commit_action()
	return true


func remove_furniture(id: String) -> bool:
	var item := get_furniture(id)
	if item == null:
		return false
	var index := project.furniture.find(item)
	_history.create_action("Supprimer %s" % item.name)
	_history.add_do_method(_delete.bind(id))
	_history.add_undo_method(_insert.bind(item.copy(), index))
	_history.commit_action()
	return true


## Adds a copy of the item, offset by DUPLICATE_OFFSET. Returns the new id, or
## "" for an unknown id.
func duplicate_furniture(id: String) -> String:
	var item := get_furniture(id)
	if item == null:
		return ""
	var clone := item.copy()
	clone.id = next_furniture_id()
	clone.pos += DUPLICATE_OFFSET
	return clone.id if add_furniture(clone) else ""


func undo() -> bool:
	return _history.undo()


func redo() -> bool:
	return _history.redo()


func can_undo() -> bool:
	return _history.has_undo()


func can_redo() -> bool:
	return _history.has_redo()


func _replace(new_project_data: ProjectData, path: String) -> void:
	project = new_project_data
	current_path = path
	_history.clear_history()
	_saved_version = _history.get_version()
	project_loaded.emit()
	path_changed.emit(path)
	dirty_changed.emit(false)


func _on_version_changed() -> void:
	edited.emit()
	dirty_changed.emit(is_dirty())


static func _write_atomically(path: String, text: String) -> Error:
	var temporary := path + ".tmp"
	var file := FileAccess.open(temporary, FileAccess.WRITE)
	if file == null:
		return FileAccess.get_open_error()
	file.store_string(text)
	file.close()
	var error := DirAccess.rename_absolute(temporary, path)
	if error != OK:
		DirAccess.remove_absolute(temporary)
	return error


func _insert(item: FurnitureItem, index: int) -> void:
	project.furniture.insert(mini(index, project.furniture.size()), item.copy())
	furniture_added.emit(item.id)


func _delete(id: String) -> void:
	project.furniture.erase(get_furniture(id))
	furniture_removed.emit(id)


func _apply(id: String, values: Dictionary) -> void:
	var item := get_furniture(id)
	for key in values:
		item.set(key, values[key])
	furniture_changed.emit(id)


func _is_valid(item: FurnitureItem) -> bool:
	var probe := ProjectData.new()
	probe.furniture.append(item)
	return ProjectValidator.validate(probe).is_empty()
```

```gdscript
class_name Autosave
extends Node
## Keeps a copy of unsaved work in user://, written DELAY_SECONDS after the
## last edit, so a crash or a forced quit never loses it. The copy is removed
## as soon as the project is saved or replaced.

const PROJECT_PATH := "user://autosave.json"
const META_PATH := "user://autosave.cfg"
const DELAY_SECONDS := 2.0
const SECTION := "autosave"

var store: Node
var project_path := PROJECT_PATH
var meta_path := META_PATH
var delay := DELAY_SECONDS
var _timer: Timer


func _init() -> void:
	name = "Autosave"
	_timer = Timer.new()
	_timer.one_shot = true
	_timer.timeout.connect(flush)
	add_child(_timer)


## Starts watching the store. Call it only after deciding what to do with a
## pending copy: replacing the project clears the copy.
func start(p_store: Node) -> void:
	store = p_store
	if not store.edited.is_connected(_on_edited):
		store.edited.connect(_on_edited)
		store.dirty_changed.connect(_on_dirty_changed)


func has_pending() -> bool:
	return FileAccess.file_exists(project_path)


## The file the pending copy was a modified version of ("" if untitled).
func pending_source_path() -> String:
	var meta := ConfigFile.new()
	if meta.load(meta_path) != OK:
		return ""
	return str(meta.get_value(SECTION, "source_path", ""))


func is_scheduled() -> bool:
	return not _timer.is_stopped()


## Writes the copy now. Returns the write error, if any.
func flush() -> Error:
	_timer.stop()
	var file := FileAccess.open(project_path, FileAccess.WRITE)
	if file == null:
		return FileAccess.get_open_error()
	file.store_string(ProjectSerializer.to_json(store.project))
	file.close()
	var meta := ConfigFile.new()
	meta.set_value(SECTION, "source_path", store.current_path)
	meta.set_value(SECTION, "saved_at", Time.get_unix_time_from_system())
	return meta.save(meta_path)


## Loads the pending copy into `target` (the store, before start()).
func restore_into(target: Node) -> PackedStringArray:
	if not has_pending():
		return PackedStringArray(["aucune sauvegarde automatique"])
	return target.restore_from_text(FileAccess.get_file_as_string(project_path), pending_source_path())


func clear() -> void:
	_timer.stop()
	for path in [project_path, meta_path]:
		if FileAccess.file_exists(path):
			DirAccess.remove_absolute(ProjectSettings.globalize_path(path))


func _on_edited() -> void:
	if store.is_dirty():
		_timer.start(delay)


func _on_dirty_changed(dirty: bool) -> void:
	if not dirty:
		clear()
```

```gdscript
class_name UiSettings
extends RefCounted
## Per-user preferences (interface zoom, recent projects), stored in a small
## config file.

const DEFAULT_PATH := "user://settings.cfg"
const SECTION := "interface"
const FILES_SECTION := "files"
const MIN_ZOOM := 0.7
const MAX_ZOOM := 1.6
const ZOOM_STEP := 0.1
const MAX_RECENT := 8

var zoom := 1.0
## Most recent first: [{"path": String, "opened_at": float (unix seconds)}].
var recent_projects: Array[Dictionary] = []


static func load_from(path: String = DEFAULT_PATH) -> UiSettings:
	var settings := UiSettings.new()
	var file := ConfigFile.new()
	if file.load(path) != OK:
		return settings
	var value = file.get_value(SECTION, "zoom", 1.0)
	if typeof(value) == TYPE_FLOAT or typeof(value) == TYPE_INT:
		settings.zoom = _clamped(value)
	var recents = file.get_value(FILES_SECTION, "recent_projects", [])
	if typeof(recents) == TYPE_ARRAY:
		for entry in recents:
			if typeof(entry) == TYPE_DICTIONARY and typeof(entry.get("path")) == TYPE_STRING \
					and typeof(entry.get("opened_at")) in [TYPE_FLOAT, TYPE_INT]:
				settings.recent_projects.append({"path": entry.path, "opened_at": float(entry.opened_at)})
		settings.recent_projects = settings.recent_projects.slice(0, MAX_RECENT)
	return settings


func save_to(path: String = DEFAULT_PATH) -> Error:
	var file := ConfigFile.new()
	file.set_value(SECTION, "zoom", zoom)
	file.set_value(FILES_SECTION, "recent_projects", recent_projects)
	return file.save(path)


## Puts `path` first in the recent list (moving it if already there), keeping
## at most MAX_RECENT entries.
func remember_project(path: String, opened_at: float = Time.get_unix_time_from_system()) -> void:
	forget_project(path)
	recent_projects.push_front({"path": path, "opened_at": opened_at})
	recent_projects = recent_projects.slice(0, MAX_RECENT)


func forget_project(path: String) -> void:
	recent_projects = recent_projects.filter(func(entry: Dictionary) -> bool: return entry.path != path)


func zoom_in() -> void:
	zoom = _clamped(zoom + ZOOM_STEP)


func zoom_out() -> void:
	zoom = _clamped(zoom - ZOOM_STEP)


func reset_zoom() -> void:
	zoom = 1.0


## Interface scale for a screen whose backing scale is `screen_scale`
## (2 on a Retina display); a non-positive scale counts as 1.
func ui_scale(screen_scale: float) -> float:
	return (screen_scale if screen_scale > 0.0 else 1.0) * zoom


## Clamped to the limits and rounded to the step, so repeated steps never drift.
static func _clamped(value: float) -> float:
	return clampf(snappedf(value, ZOOM_STEP), MIN_ZOOM, MAX_ZOOM)
```

```gdscript
## &"toggle_inspector", &"help", &"cutaway", &"zoom_in", &"zoom_out",
## &"zoom_reset", &"welcome", &"open", &"save", &"save_as", or &"" for none. Accepts the QWERTY and AZERTY variants.
static func action_for(event: InputEventKey) -> StringName:
	if not event.pressed or event.echo:
		return &""
	var character := char(event.unicode) if event.unicode > 0 else ""
	if event.is_command_or_control_pressed():
		if event.keycode == KEY_O:
			return &"welcome" if event.shift_pressed else &"open"
		if event.keycode == KEY_S:
			return &"save_as" if event.shift_pressed else &"save"
		if character in ["+", "="] or event.keycode in [KEY_PLUS, KEY_EQUAL, KEY_KP_ADD]:
			return &"zoom_in"
		if character == "-" or event.keycode in [KEY_MINUS, KEY_KP_SUBTRACT]:
			return &"zoom_out"
		if character == "0" or event.keycode in [KEY_0, KEY_KP_0] or event.physical_keycode == KEY_0:
			return &"zoom_reset"
		return &""
	if event.alt_pressed:
		return &""
	if character == "?" or event.keycode == KEY_F1:
		return &"help"
	match event.keycode:
		KEY_C:
			return &"toggle_catalog"
		KEY_I:
			return &"toggle_inspector"
		KEY_H:
			return &"cutaway"
	return &""
```

```gdscript
extends Node
## File menu behaviour: welcome page, new, open, save, save as, restore after
## a crash, confirmation before losing changes, window title. Talks to the
## store and the autosave; asks the user through the welcome page and dialogs.

enum MenuId { WELCOME, NEW_EMPTY, NEW_FROM_TEMPLATE, OPEN, SAVE, SAVE_AS }

const APP_NAME := "3D Appartment"
const UNTITLED := "Sans titre"
const TEMPLATE_PATH := "res://data/log16.json"
const EXTENSION := "json"
const FILE_FILTER := "*.json ; Projets 3D Appartment"
const UNSAVED_HINT := "Vos modifications restent en mémoire et dans la sauvegarde automatique."

var store: Node
var autosave: Autosave
var settings: UiSettings
var welcome: WelcomeScreen
var settings_path := UiSettings.DEFAULT_PATH

## Tests replace it; by default it opens the system file dialog.

## Called once the user agreed to quit; tests replace it.

var _error: AcceptDialog

## Whether a project has been opened since launch (the welcome page can be closed).

func _init() -> void:

func setup(p_store: Node, p_autosave: Autosave, p_settings: UiSettings, p_settings_path: String,
		p_welcome: WelcomeScreen) -> void:
	store = p_store
	autosave = p_autosave
	settings = p_settings
	settings_path = p_settings_path
	welcome = p_welcome
	welcome.recent_chosen.connect(open_recent)
	welcome.recent_forgotten.connect(forget_recent)
	welcome.new_empty_chosen.connect(new_empty)

	var restore_name := ""

func forget_recent(path: String) -> void:

	_save_settings()
	show_welcome()

	_confirm_discard(quit_action)

func answer_confirmation(choice: StringName) -> void:
	_confirm.hide()

	_after_confirm = Callable()
	match choice:
		&"save":
			_save_then(then)
		&"discard":
			autosave.clear()
			then.call()

		if _report("Restauration impossible", autosave.restore_into(store)):
			_project_ready()
			return
	autosave.clear()
	show_welcome()

		_remember(path)
		_project_ready()

func _project_ready() -> void:
	_has_project = true
	welcome.close()
	autosave.start(store)


## Saves to the current file, or asks for one; then runs `then` on success.
func _save_then(then: Callable) -> void:
	if store.current_path != "":
		if _save_to(store.current_path) and then.is_valid():
			then.call()
		return
	path_picker.call(FileDialog.FILE_MODE_SAVE_FILE, func(path: String) -> void:
		if _save_to(path) and then.is_valid():
			then.call())


func _save_to(path: String) -> bool:
	if path.get_extension().to_lower() != EXTENSION:
		path += "." + EXTENSION
	var errors: PackedStringArray = store.save_to_file(path)
	if not errors.is_empty():
		errors.append(UNSAVED_HINT)
		_report("Enregistrement impossible", errors)
		return false
	_remember(path)
	return true


## Runs `then` now if nothing would be lost, otherwise asks first.
func _confirm_discard(then: Callable) -> void:
	if not store.is_dirty():
		then.call()
		return
	_after_confirm = then
	var file: String = store.current_path.get_file() if store.current_path != "" else UNTITLED
	_confirm.dialog_text = "Enregistrer les modifications de « %s » ?" % file
	_confirm.popup_centered()


## Shows the errors, if any. Returns true when there were none.
func _report(title: String, errors: PackedStringArray) -> bool:
	if errors.is_empty():
		return true
	_error.title = title
	_error.dialog_text = "\n".join(errors)
	_error.popup_centered()
	return false


func _remember(path: String) -> void:
	settings.remember_project(path)
	_save_settings()


func _save_settings() -> void:
	var error := settings.save_to(settings_path)
	if error != OK:
		push_warning("Préférences non enregistrées (%s) : %s" % [settings_path, error_string(error)])


func _on_state_changed() -> void:
	if is_inside_tree():
		get_window().title = title_text()


func _pick_with_dialog(mode: FileDialog.FileMode, done: Callable) -> void:
	_file_dialog.file_mode = mode
	_file_dialog.title = "Ouvrir un projet" if mode == FileDialog.FILE_MODE_OPEN_FILE else "Enregistrer le projet"
	var folder: String = store.current_path.get_base_dir() if store.current_path.begins_with("/") else ""

	for connection in _file_dialog.file_selected.get_connections():
		_file_dialog.file_selected.disconnect(connection.callable)
	_file_dialog.file_selected.connect(done, CONNECT_ONE_SHOT)
	_file_dialog.popup_centered_ratio(0.6)


func _build_dialogs() -> void:
	_confirm = ConfirmationDialog.new()
	_confirm.name = "ConfirmDiscard"
	_confirm.title = APP_NAME
	_confirm.ok_button_text = "Enregistrer"

	add_child(_confirm)
	_error = AcceptDialog.new()
	_error.name = "Error"
	add_child(_error)
	_file_dialog = FileDialog.new()
	_file_dialog.name = "FileDialog"
	_file_dialog.access = FileDialog.ACCESS_FILESYSTEM
	_file_dialog.use_native_dialog = true
	_file_dialog.filters = PackedStringArray([FILE_FILTER])
	add_child(_file_dialog)
```

```gdscript
## Application root: lighting, camera, the 3D view of the open project, the
## furniture tool, the collapsible side panels, the shortcuts help and the
## File menu (with autosave).

const SUN_ROTATION_DEG := Vector3(-50, -40, 0)
const SUN_COLOR := Color("#ffe6c7")

## Where preferences and the autosave are kept; tests point them elsewhere.
var settings_path := UiSettings.DEFAULT_PATH
var autosave_project_path := Autosave.PROJECT_PATH
var autosave_meta_path := Autosave.META_PATH

var _view: WorldView
var _camera: OrbitCamera
var _furniture: FurnitureView
var _tool: FurnitureTool
var _settings: UiSettings

var _catalog_side: CollapsibleSide
var _inspector_side: CollapsibleSide

var _autosave: Autosave

func _ready() -> void:
	_settings = UiSettings.load_from(settings_path)
	# Closing the window asks first when there are unsaved changes.
	get_tree().auto_accept_quit = false
	add_child(_environment())
	add_child(_sun())
	_view = WorldView.new()
	_view.name = "World"
	add_child(_view)
	_furniture = FurnitureView.new()
	_furniture.name = "Furniture"

	_camera = OrbitCamera.new()
	_camera.name = "Camera"
	add_child(_camera)

	# first, so the tool can claim clicks and Shift + wheel before the camera.
	_tool = FurnitureTool.new()
	_tool.name = "FurnitureTool"
	add_child(_tool)
	_furniture.bind_store(ProjectStore)
	_tool.setup(ProjectStore, _furniture, _camera)
	_autosave = Autosave.new()
	_autosave.project_path = autosave_project_path
	_autosave.meta_path = autosave_meta_path

	add_child(_files)

	ProjectStore.project_loaded.connect(_on_project_loaded)
	_files.startup()


func _exit_tree() -> void:
	get_tree().auto_accept_quit = true


func _notification(what: int) -> void:
	if what == NOTIFICATION_WM_CLOSE_REQUEST and _files != null:
		_files.request_quit()


func _unhandled_key_input(event: InputEvent) -> void:
	match Shortcuts.action_for(event as InputEventKey):
		&"cutaway":
			_view.cycle_cutaway_mode()
		&"toggle_catalog":
			_catalog_side.toggle()
		&"toggle_inspector":
			_inspector_side.toggle()
		&"help":
			_help.toggle()
		&"welcome":
			_files.show_welcome()
		&"open":
			_files.open()
		&"save":
			_files.save()
		&"save_as":
			_files.save_as()
		&"zoom_in":
			_change_zoom(_settings.zoom_in)
		&"zoom_out":
			_change_zoom(_settings.zoom_out)
		&"zoom_reset":
			_change_zoom(_settings.reset_zoom)
		_:
			return
	get_viewport().set_input_as_handled()

## user zoom, so panels keep the size of other apps whatever resolution macOS uses.
func ui_scale() -> float:

## out at the scaled size and rasterises fonts at full resolution (sharp text).

	get_window().content_scale_factor = ui_scale()
```

````markdown
### Fichiers

Au lancement, une **page d'accueil** permet de choisir le projet :
````
